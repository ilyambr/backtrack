# Privacy Policy for Backtrack

**Last updated:** August 2026

Backtrack and the Backtrack Stream Deck Plugin are designed with user privacy as a core principle.

### 1. No Data Collection
Backtrack does not collect, log, track, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or analytics.

### 2. Local-Only Processing
All communication between Backtrack and its Stream Deck plugin occurs exclusively over your local computer (`127.0.0.1` loopback IPC). No data or commands are sent over the public internet.

### 3. Media & Storage
All screen recordings, replay buffers, audio files, bookmarks, and video clips remain 100% on your local storage devices and are never uploaded to any cloud servers.

### 4. Open Source
Backtrack is fully open source. You can inspect the source code and networking implementation at [https://github.com/ilyambr/backtrack](https://github.com/ilyambr/backtrack).
